# About us

XCA is an academic and technology student association at Xi'an University of Finance and Economics, providing space for students of different experience levels to learn, discuss, and collaborate.
::

::section{.about-values .neoverse-prose}

## Community values

**Sincerity, inclusion, harmony, passion**

Equal conversation, mutual respect, and shared knowledge guide the community. Students at different learning stages and with different technical interests are welcome.
::

::section{.about-topic .neoverse-prose}

## Technical discussion and knowledge sharing

Discuss computing questions and share experience. Build understanding through conversation and give the ideas developed along the way a place to be shared.
::

::section{.about-topic .neoverse-prose}

## Hands-on projects and open-source collaboration

Projects are not limited to a particular technology stack. They can address practical needs or explore technical ideas. Contribute to an existing project or propose a new one and develop it through discussion and collaboration.
::

::section{.about-participate .neoverse-prose}

## Start with a contribution

Contributing does not require building a project alone. Documentation fixes, issue reproduction, and feedback are valuable contributions too.
